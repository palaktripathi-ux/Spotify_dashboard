Spotify Analytics Dashboard

An interactive dashboard for exploring song and artist data from Spotify, covering catalog composition, popularity trends, explicit-content mix, and top artists/tracks.

Overview

The dashboard opens on an Overview tab (alongside Home, Artists, and Songs) and gives a single-screen summary of the song catalog: how many distinct songs and artists are tracked, how popularity and release volume trend over time, and which artists and tracks dominate the dataset.

Layout
Navigation

Top bar with the Spotify logo and four tabs — Home, Overview, Artists, Songs — for drilling into more detail than the summary view provides.

Songs and Artists (left rail)

A scrollable list of tracks with cover art, track title, and artist name (e.g. "Slut!" (Taylor's Version) — Taylor Swift, 16 CARRIAGES — Beyoncé, 360 — Charli XCX), acting as a quick browse/reference panel alongside the aggregate charts.

Key metrics (stat tiles)

Four headline numbers summarizing the full catalog:

Distinct Songs — total unique tracks (789)
Count of Artist — total unique artists (342)
Avg Duration — average track length in minutes (3.28)
Avg Popularity — average Spotify popularity score (90)
Now Playing

A media-player-style card showing the current track's album art (e.g. Dua Lipa — Houdini) with playback controls (previous, play/pause, next).

Catalog breakdown (donut charts)

Four donut charts comparing category splits:

Songs by Album Type — single vs. album counts
Explicit and Non-Explicit — explicit vs. non-explicit song counts
Distinct Songs by Year — catalog growth by release year (e.g. 2023 vs. 2024)
Avg Popularity by Album Type — average popularity for compilation, single, and album releases
Avg Popularity by Month

A line chart tracking average popularity score across all 12 months, with a toggle to switch the time grain between Month and Quarter.

Songs by Artist (bar charts)

Two horizontal bar charts ranking artists:

By song count in the catalog (e.g. Taylor Swift, Travis Scott, Drake, Bad Bunny)
By stream/play count for individual top tracks (e.g. I Wanna Be..., Cruel Summer, As It Was, One of The...)
Distinct Songs by Month

A column chart showing how many distinct songs were added/released each month, with peak months (e.g. Jul, Oct, Nov) highlighted.
